Help & Support

Hi! I'm Aqualo, your tiny water buddy. Here are answers to common questions. If you can't find yours, just write to us.

Write to us

Questions, a bug, an idea, or just saying hi. We read every message and usually reply within a few days.

Questions

How is my daily goal calculated?

Aqualo starts from about 33 ml per kg of body weight and adds a little for an active day (+350 ml) or workouts (+700 ml). On hot or humid days, the weather card may suggest a bit extra for that day only.

You can change your goal any time: tap Goal on the home screen. Your body info is under Profile → My info.

Why don't all drinks count the same?

Each drink has a hydration factor based on how well it hydrates compared with water. Milk-based drinks, for example, count a little more than their volume. Tea and coffee count like water in normal amounts. Tap and hold a drink in the drink list to see its details.

My reminders don't arrive
  • Check that notifications are allowed for Aqualo in your phone's settings.
  • Focus / Do Not Disturb modes can hide them.
  • On Android, battery optimization can stop them. Set Aqualo to "Unrestricted" in the battery settings.
  • Open Profile → Reminders and check that reminders are on and the hours are right.
How do streaks work?

Each day you reach your goal adds one day to your streak. If you need a break, vacation mode (Aqualo Plus) freezes your streak so it doesn't break. If a streak does break, you may be able to rescue it for a short time.

How do I add a friend?

Share your Aqualo ID (Profile, under your name) with your friend. They go to Friends → Add and enter it, then you accept the request. When you both reach your goals on the same day, your mutual streak grows. It starts after 3 days together.

Friends see only your name, avatar, today's progress as a percentage and your streaks. They don't see how much you drank.

How do I change the language or unit?

Go to Profile → Preferences. Aqualo speaks English, Turkish, German, Japanese, Korean, French, Portuguese (Brazil), Spanish (Latin America), Italian and Traditional Chinese, and supports ml or oz.

How do I cancel or restore Aqualo Plus?
  • iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Aqualo.
  • Android: Google Play → profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Aqualo.

Deleting the app doesn't cancel the subscription. To restore it on a new phone, open the Aqualo Plus screen and tap Restore. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google.

I got a new phone. Will my data move?

Your log is stored on your phone. It moves only if you restore your new phone from a backup of the old one (iCloud or Google). Friends and mutual streaks are on our server. If "Protect your account" is available in Profile, link Apple or Google on your old phone to take them with you.

How do I delete my data?

See Delete your data. In short: Profile → Delete social account removes everything from our server, and deleting the app removes everything on your phone.
