Delete your data

How to delete your Aqualo account and data.

Last updated: 29 September 2026

In the app (instant)

  1. Open Aqualo and go to the Profile tab.
  2. In the Account section, tap Delete social account.
  3. Confirm. Your social account is deleted from our server right away.

To also remove everything stored on your phone (your log, goals and settings), delete the Aqualo app.

By email

If you no longer have the app, email aqualo.destek@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Aqualo data". Include your Aqualo ID if you know it (for example AQ-7K2M-9QXF). We'll delete your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile: Aqualo ID, display name, avatar number, time zone and language
  • Your daily summaries (ml and goal per day), streak, vacation periods and last drink time
  • Friendships, friend requests, nudges, mutual streaks and restores
  • Your notification token and any linked Apple or Google sign-in

What may be kept

  • Encrypted server backups are overwritten automatically within a short time.
  • Purchase records kept by Apple or Google, and any records we're legally required to keep.
  • Anonymous analytics that can't be linked back to you.

Deleting your data doesn't cancel an Aqualo Plus subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play settings.
