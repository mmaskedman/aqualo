Privacy Policy

What Aqualo keeps, why, and how you stay in control.

Last updated: 6 October 2026

The short version

  • Your water log stays on your phone. What you drink, your goals, your weight and activity level are stored only on your device. We don't receive them.
  • No sign-up needed. The Friends feature uses an anonymous account. Friends see only your name, avatar, today's progress as a percentage, your streak and whether you just had a drink.
  • Location is only for the weather, rounded to about 1 km, and we never store it.
  • You can delete your social account any time in the app: Profile → Delete social account.
  • The app has no ads. No data is shared with any ad network.
  • We don't sell your data.

Aqualo ("the app") is a water tracking app made by Yunus Yılmaz, an independent developer based in Türkiye ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles, why, who helps us run it, and the choices you have. For the purposes of the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK), we are the data controller.

Questions? Write to aqualo.destek@gmail.com.

1. Information that stays on your phone

Aqualo stores the following only on your device, in the app's own storage:

  • the drinks you log (type, amount, time), your daily goals, streaks, points and ranks;
  • the body information you enter to calculate your goal (weight and activity level);
  • your reminder times, app settings, language and unit;
  • the name and avatar or photo you choose for your profile.

We don't upload this information to our servers, except for the small summary described in section 2 if you use Friends. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it. Your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google) may include it, under Apple's or Google's terms.

Aqualo is a wellness habit app. It doesn't read data from Apple Health or Google Health Connect and doesn't collect medical records.

2. Friends and your social account

To let you add friends and keep mutual streaks, the app creates an anonymous account for you on our server. You don't give us an email address or phone number.

What the server keeps

  • Profile: your Aqualo ID (for example AQ-7K2M-9QXF), the display name you choose, the number of the preset avatar you picked, your time zone and app language.
  • Daily summary (sent only when you have friends or pending friend requests): for each of the last 14 days, how much you drank in ml and that day's goal; your personal streak; vacation periods; and the time of your last drink.
  • Social activity: friend requests, friendships, nudges, mutual streaks and streak restores.

What your friends can see

Your name, avatar, today's progress as a percentage, your personal streak, your mutual streak with them, and whether you had a drink in the last ~25 minutes. They can't see how many ml you drank, your goal, which drinks you had, your weight, your location or your photo.

Anyone who knows your Aqualo ID can send you a friend request. Nothing is shared with them unless you accept.

Linking Apple or Google (optional)

If this option is available and you choose to link your account to Sign in with Apple or Google, we receive a unique account identifier from Apple or Google and, if you allow it, your email address. We use it only to bring your social account back if you change phones.

Deleting your social account

In the app, go to Profile → Delete social account. Your profile, daily summaries, friendships, requests, nudges and mutual streaks are deleted from our database right away. See also Delete your data.

The server is provided by Supabase. Our database is hosted in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany).

3. Location and weather

If you allow it, Aqualo uses your approximate location to show the local weather and suggest a little extra water on hot or humid days. The coordinates are rounded to two decimal places (about 1 km) and sent to the free weather service Open-Meteo, which returns the forecast. We don't store your location, and it's never sent to our server or to your friends. Location is optional; the app works without it.

4. Notifications

Water reminders are scheduled on your phone by the app itself. No server is involved and no one else can see them. You can turn them off in the app or in your phone's settings.

When notifications about friend activity (for example a nudge) are available, the app will store a push token with your social account so we can deliver them through Expo's push service and Apple Push Notification service or Firebase Cloud Messaging. The token is deleted with your social account.

5. Photos and sharing

If you choose a profile photo, the app asks for permission to open your photo library and uses only the photo you pick. The photo stays on your phone; we don't upload it.

Recap and profile cards are created on your phone. They're shared only if you choose to share them through your phone's share menu.

6. Aqualo Plus and purchases

Payments are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment card details.

  • RevenueCat checks whether you have an active subscription. It receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase receipts and basic device and app information.
  • Superwall shows the subscription screen. It receives an anonymous ID, basic device information and how you interact with that screen.

7. Usage analytics and crash reports

To understand which features help and to fix problems, the app sends usage events to Mixpanel, for example which screens are opened and when a drink is logged.

  • Events come with the device model, OS and app version, language and an approximate country or region.
  • They're linked to a random ID, not to your name.
  • When the app crashes, a crash report with technical details (device, OS, app version and error log) is sent so we can fix the bug.

Where the law requires consent, we ask before collecting analytics. You can withdraw that consent at any time in the app's settings.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR, KVKK article 11 or the California CCPA/CPRA), you have the right to:

  • know whether we process your data and get a copy of it;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data deleted;
  • restrict or object to certain processing;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw your consent at any time;
  • complain to a data protection authority.

For the complaint route: in Türkiye this is the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK). In the EU/UK it's your local supervisory authority.

Most of your data never leaves your phone, so you already control it. For anything on our server, email aqualo.destek@gmail.com with your Aqualo ID (Profile, under your name) so we can find your account without asking for anything else. We reply within 30 days. We won't treat you differently for using these rights.

13. Children

Aqualo isn't intended for children under 13, or under the minimum age required in your country to use online services without parental consent. We don't knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created a social account, contact us and we'll delete it.

14. Security

All connections to our server and providers are encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). The database only accepts requests through checked functions: each user can reach only their own data and the limited friend summary described above. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and to keep as little of it as possible.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The date at the top shows the latest version. If a change is significant, we'll let you know in the app before it takes effect.
